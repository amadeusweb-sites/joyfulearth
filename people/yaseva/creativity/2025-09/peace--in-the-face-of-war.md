The Extreme Right-Wing Zionists,
bringing harmony on Israel Nation,
a nation of great antiquity,
chosen people of the Most High,
God of Jacob, Isaac, and Abraham,
God of mercy, compassion, and love.

Hard-hearted Zionists,
misguided spiritualists,
violent land grabbers with drones and missiles,
unleash genocide on Palestinians.

Nations are aghast at Bibi's blind pilgrims
coveting Palestinian land to expand Israel's
borders by hook or crook.

Rearing at these vile Israeli leaders,
all ye nations with hearts unable to see the
horror and misery heaped on Palestinian civilians.

Quarantine Israeli tourists who have come to their nations,
hold them as soft hostages till Bibi Haas,
his sordid crime, made peace prevailed for Palestinians.
